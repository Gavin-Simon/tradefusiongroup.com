Privacy Notice

Effective date: 24 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your information

The data controller for information collected through this website is Trade Fusion Group (TRADE FUSION GROUP LTD, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17168889). Its registered office address is shown on our Company information page.

Contact: info@tradefusiongroup.com

2. What we collect

This website has no contact forms, accounts, analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not set cookies of its own. We receive information when you email us, including when you use an email button on this site, which opens your own email application. That information is your name, email address, company, the content of your message and anything else you choose to include.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process technical information such as your IP address when you visit. Please see GitHub's privacy statement. The fonts used on this site are hosted on our own site, not by a third party.

3. Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, discuss your requirements, prepare proposals and manage any resulting relationship. Where UK data protection law applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and building business relationships, and, where you ask us to take steps before entering into a contract, on taking those steps.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. Emails sent to us are handled by our email provider and by members of our team and subcontracted specialists who help us respond to enquiries, who are bound by confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Our team works from several countries, including the United Kingdom, India and the United States, so your information may be accessed from outside the UK. Where this happens, we put in place the safeguards that UK data protection law requires. Contact us if you would like more detail.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. If we enter into a contract with you, we keep records for as long as needed for that contract and to meet our legal obligations.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to our use of it, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email us at the address above. Other laws, such as EU or US state laws, may give you similar rights, and we will respect them where they apply.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please email us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113).
