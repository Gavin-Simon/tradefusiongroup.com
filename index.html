Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. The initial consultation is free and takes approximately 15–20 minutes.

Is the 30-day pilot free?

Eligible businesses may qualify for a complimentary 30-day pilot, subject to agreed service requirements, resource availability and written terms. Eligibility, the services included, resource limits and conditions are confirmed in writing before any pilot begins. Not every enquiry qualifies, and a pilot does not include unlimited work. Paid pilots and ongoing managed support are also available.

How can outsourcing help manage our support costs?

FusionOps can help reduce the need for additional in-house recruitment, training and day-to-day supervision, and can scale capacity with your workload instead of adding fixed staffing overheads. We tailor the delivery model to your workload, coverage requirements and budget. We do not quote savings percentages, because the right model depends on your situation.

Which industries do you support?

Our services support businesses across many industries. Ecommerce and gaming are sectors where we have relevant experience, and we scope each engagement around your own workflows.

How do you communicate with customers?

Our support is delivered through chat and email. We do not provide voice support.

Which platforms does your team work in?

Our team has demonstrated experience with Gorgias, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart and Discord for support and ecommerce, and Asana, Microsoft Teams, Trello and Microsoft Project for project work. After onboarding, we also support client-hosted or in-house systems and other platforms. See the full platform list.

How quickly can you start, and what coverage hours do you offer?

Onboarding timelines are agreed based on your requirements, team availability, training and system access. Coverage hours, including 24/7 where required, are agreed per engagement.

How is quality managed?

Our QA process follows a cycle of review, feedback, coaching and improvement, with weekly QA reviews and reporting. Staffing, reporting frequency and service levels are confirmed in each written agreement.

Will you share client names?

Our clients' identities are confidential, so we do not publish client names, logos or results. We are happy to discuss relevant experience in general terms on a call.

Who will I speak with?

A specialist from our team will reply to your email. On project engagements, delivery is led by PMP-certified project managers.

Where is the team based?

Our specialists work remotely from several countries, including India. We coordinate working hours to suit your United States or United Kingdom business day.

How are access and confidentiality handled?

Access arrangements and confidentiality terms are agreed in writing before work starts, and the team works within the systems and permissions you grant.
