About FusionOps by Trade Fusion Group

An international customer experience, BPO and managed business operations partner.

Who we are

A managed operations partner

FusionOps by Trade Fusion Group provides customer support outsourcing, ecommerce operations, gaming technical support, quality assurance, project management and business process support for businesses across industries.

FusionOps by Trade Fusion Group supports international businesses through our operations and business presence across the United States, United Kingdom and India. Contracting arrangements are confirmed individually according to each client's requirements.

Why we exist

Dependable capacity for growing businesses

Many growing businesses need dependable support and operations capacity before they are ready to build a large in-house team. We provide experienced people, structured workflows and dedicated management, so quality does not slip and fixed staffing overheads stay under control while the business grows.

Ecommerce and retail

Support and operations behind your store and marketplaces.

Gaming and digital

Player-facing support delivered through agreed workflows.

Other industries

Any business with repeatable support or operational workflows.

How we work

Clear communication. Accountable delivery. Consistent quality.

Clear communication

Scope, coverage and success measures are agreed with you in writing, and progress is reported regularly.

Accountable delivery

Dedicated management oversees each engagement, and PMP-certified project managers lead delivery on project engagements.

Consistent quality

A review, feedback, coaching and improvement cycle, with weekly QA reviews and reporting.

Our UK business location is London, United Kingdom. Our specialists work remotely from several countries, including India, and coordinate working hours to suit your United States or United Kingdom business day. Client identities are confidential, so we do not publish client names, logos or results.
