About us

About Trade Fusion Group

Trade Fusion Group is an international business registered in the United Kingdom, the United States and India. Through two divisions, FusionOps and Import & Export, we support businesses with customer service and operations, and with cross-border trade in industrial materials.

Why we exist

Closing the gap between ambition and capacity

Many businesses want to grow but face the cost, time and know-how involved in building support and operations teams, or in finding trading partners abroad. Trade Fusion Group exists to help close that gap, combining international business relationships with careful, quality-focused delivery.

Who we serve

Businesses in the UK, the US and beyond

We support startups, growing businesses and established organisations in the UK, the US and internationally. FusionOps is built for ecommerce brands, gaming companies and any business that needs customer service or operational support. Import & Export serves industrial buyers and suppliers.

How we work

Two divisions, one group

Each division has its own focus and its own way of working.

FusionOps by Trade Fusion Group

Customer service, gaming player support, ecommerce operations, quality assurance and remote business support. An engagement can be a defined project, an ongoing arrangement or a team built around your needs, with training, quality monitoring and operational oversight. Coverage hours, including round-the-clock coverage where required, are agreed for each engagement.

Import & Export

We connect industrial suppliers and buyers, with a focus on zinc by-products, zinc-related materials and international sourcing, supporting commercial coordination and cross-border trade requirements.

Our approach

Scoped to your requirements

We work toward long-term partnerships based on transparency and accountability. Every engagement is scoped to your requirements, whether that starts with a small pilot, a dedicated support team or an international trading opportunity. A scoped 30-day pilot is available for suitable FusionOps projects. Scope, fees and success measures are agreed in advance, and nothing is guaranteed beyond what is written in your agreement.

Our mission

To help businesses grow by connecting them with skilled people, efficient operations and international commercial opportunities.

Our vision

To be an international business partner known for quality, reliability and long-term value.
